# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-08-01

## Who We Are

24edu-demo is an internal demonstration project operated by its owner. It is an AI-powered university information chatbot that lets prospective students chat with an AI assistant and request a live human agent. The project is hosted on Vercel and uses Upstash for data storage.

## What Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

- **Identity data** — first and last name
- **Contact data** — email address and phone number (including country dialling code)
- **Demographic data** — country or nationality
- **Educational data** — current education level, intended programme of study, and intended intake (month and year)
- **Session data** — a randomly generated guest identifier (not tied to your real identity), your chat messages, and basic device information such as browser and operating system, used to associate a session with you and support live sessions

This data is submitted voluntarily through the lead capture form before a chat session begins. Guests may also update their identity, contact, demographic, and educational data at any time from their profile page.

We do not collect payment information, run analytics scripts, or use third-party tracking tools. Your theme preference (light or dark mode) is stored only in your browser and is never sent to us.

## How We Use Your Data

| Purpose | Legal basis (GDPR Art. 6) |
|---|---|
| Routing your chat session and connecting you to a human agent when requested | Art. 6(1)(b) — necessary to perform the service you requested |
| Generating AI responses to your questions, including web search grounding for education-related queries | Art. 6(1)(b) — necessary to perform the service you requested |
| Displaying your contact details and messages to admin staff during a live session | Art. 6(1)(b) — necessary to perform the service you requested |
| Storing session history so you can resume a previous conversation | Art. 6(1)(b) — necessary to perform the service you requested |

We do not use your data for marketing, profiling, or automated decision-making.

## Data Sharing and Third Parties

Your data is processed by the following third-party services:

- **Google (Gemini API)** — chat messages, and recent conversation context, are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate AI responses. When a question is education-related and not answered by our own data, Gemini may also issue a Google Search query. Google processes this content as a data processor under its API terms.
- **Upstash** — lead data, session history, and uploaded knowledge-base content are stored in Upstash Redis and Upstash Vector (hosted on AWS infrastructure). Upstash acts as a data processor.
- **Vercel** — the application is hosted on Vercel, which processes request logs and may temporarily handle data in transit.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any other third parties.

## International Data Transfers

Data may be transferred to and stored on servers located outside the European Economic Area (EEA), including the United States, through Upstash, Google, and Vercel. These transfers rely on the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission or equivalent safeguards provided by those processors.

## Data Retention

| Data category | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Lead form data (name, email, phone, country, education details) | Until manually deleted by an admin via the Settings panel, or until all data is cleared |
| Chat session messages and device information | Until manually cleared via the Settings panel |
| Admin session tokens | 24 hours from login |

This is an internal demo project. Redis data is ephemeral and may be flushed as part of routine maintenance.

## Your Rights

Under GDPR (Articles 15–21), you have the right to:

- **Access** — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
- **Rectification** — correct inaccurate or incomplete data (you can also do this directly on the profile page)
- **Erasure** — request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Restriction** — ask us to pause processing of your data while a dispute is resolved
- **Data portability** — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
- **Object** — object to processing based on legitimate interests

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in the Contact section below.

## Cookies

The only cookie we set is `admin_token`, a session cookie used exclusively to authenticate admin users. It expires after 24 hours and is not used for tracking or analytics. Guests who use the chat interface do not receive any cookies; your guest identifier and theme preference are stored in browser local storage instead, not in a cookie.

## Children's Privacy

This service is intended for prospective university students and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe a child has submitted data through this service, please contact us and we will delete it.

## Supervisory Authority

If you are located in the EEA and believe your data has been processed unlawfully, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In the UK, that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk).
